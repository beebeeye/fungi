🍄 Forest Mushroom Hunting

The whole family is heading into the forest! This forest never ends — keep walking left or right and the woods go on forever, with mushroom houses and volcanoes appearing along the way. Climb the big trees and fill your backpack with mushrooms (100) to win. Regular mushrooms go straight into your backpack when you touch them; the forest also has chubby bolete — after you touch one, click once to stomp it, and it will spray brownish-yellow spores! More mushrooms pop up on rainy days; when thunder rolls, hurry into a giant mushroom house, or lightning will burn the mushrooms and empty your backpack! The forest also has the red-capped, white-spotted fly agaric — touch it and you will hallucinate: you’ll walk upside down, every control is reversed, and you’ll see things that aren’t there!

🎮 How to play

  • Walk, jump and climb trees around the forest, looking for mushrooms that pop up.
  • When a character spots a mushroom, they happily cover their face and gasp — no button needed: just walk into the mushroom and it goes straight into the backpack.
  • The forest also has bolete: thick stems and brown caps, and they never go in a backpack. After you touch one, click once (or tap the screen, or tap "Stomp") and your character stomps it — the bolete instantly sprays out brownish-yellow spores, then shrivels up once it's done spraying.
  • The forest also has fly agaric: a red cap with white spots — the prettiest mushroom of all, and the most dangerous! touching it causes a hallucination (about 15 s): your whole body walks upside down, the controls are all reversed (press Left and you go right, press Up and you go down, try to jump and you squat instead), and you’ll see things that aren’t there — blinding flashes, a huge colorful downpour, giant mushrooms floating in the sky, and the whole world wobbling around. Don't panic — once the hallucination passes, everything goes back to normal. Your AI partners are smart — they never touch it.
  • The collection bar holds 100 mushrooms in total. Every mushroom you pick fills it a little more, and filling it to 100 means victory.
  • The other two family members are AI partners: they find mushrooms on their own, cover their faces and gasp, and call out "There’s a mushroom here!" to you. Everyone has their own backpack: what a partner picks goes into their own backpack (the 🎒 number on their name tag is their count), and only mushrooms you pick yourself go into your backpack and fill the collection bar.
  • The forest has no edges: keep walking left or right and giant mushroom houses and volcanoes keep appearing one after another — during thunder, step into the nearest mushroom house to shelter from lightning. The strip at the top of the screen shows which way the nearest mushroom house and volcano are.
  • The game is full of sounds: footsteps pattering, landing thuds, rain rustling, birdsong on fine days, the little "pop" of a mushroom sprouting... Listen closely — before lightning strikes, you’ll first hear a low rumble — when you hear it, run for a mushroom house! The "Sound" button at the top right turns all sound effects on or off.
  • Need a break? Tap "⏸ Pause"(or press P ): the game stops at once — mushrooms, weather and lightning all freeze, and your backpack is safe; it also pauses automatically. Tap "Keep playing" to carry on.
